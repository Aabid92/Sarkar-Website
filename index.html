قَلْبٌ سَلِيمٌ

"The heart is the mirror of God. Polish it with remembrance and it shall reflect the Divine Light."

— Rabbani Peer

مَنْ عَرَفَ نَفْسَهُ

"Whoever knows himself truly, knows his Lord. The journey within is the greatest journey of all."

— Sufi Wisdom

اَللهُ نُورُ السَّمٰوَاتِ

"Die before you die — and discover that there was never any death. The soul is eternal in its beloved."

— Tasawwuf Teaching

ذِكْرُ اللهِ

"Dhikr is not merely the movement of the tongue. It is the trembling of the heart in divine awe."

— Rabbani Peer

اَلْمَحَبَّةُ

"Love is the foundation of Sufism. When love enters the heart, nothing remains but the Beloved."

— Sufi Wisdom

صِدْقُ الطَّرِيق

"Walk the path with sincerity, for sincerity is the lamp that lights every step of the spiritual journey."

— Khalifa Teaching
